Contact us ↗
What should I bring with me?

It depends on the service. Bring the relevant original documents and a copy where appropriate. Contact us first to check the current requirements for your specific application.

Can you guarantee approval of an application?

No. TAPANCSC can provide assistance where available, but eligibility checks, verification, fees and final decisions are handled by the relevant department or service provider.

How much does a service cost?

Charges depend on the service and any official portal or provider fee. Please confirm the complete amount before proceeding and ask for a receipt where applicable.

Can I ask about a service before visiting?

Yes. Use the enquiry form below to prepare a message. Add your correct contact details to the website settings before publishing so enquiries can be sent to you.

How do you handle personal information?

Share only what is necessary for your request. Never send passwords, OTPs, PINs or full payment credentials through this website. Use official channels for sensitive verification steps.
